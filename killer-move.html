Tool · Eleven Move

Killer Move ×11

Every complete B11 calculation advances its running total with ×11. First master the fixed identity 11x = 10x + x.

Go to the exercises

The “Killer Move” is simply multiplication by 11: eleven copies are ten copies plus one more. This identity is the repeated step in B11.

How it works — explained step by step

33 × 11

  1. First: 33 × 10 = 330. Enter 330 in the first field.
  2. Then: 330 + 33 = 363. Enter 363 in the final field.

Carry example: 78 × 11

  1. 78 × 10 = 780. Then 780 + 78 = 858. Carry as usual when adding; do not just insert 15 between 7 and 8.

Start with two digits, then move to three and four digits when comfortable. The rule also holds for 0 and negative numbers: 0 × 11 = 0; −3 × 11 = −30 − 3 = −33. The random trainer uses positive whole numbers.

Problem

Killer Move10x + x

For whole numbers, multiply by 10 by appending a zero, then add the original number once. Example: 33 × 11 = 330 + 33 = 363.

Numbers: . or , is a decimal separator, never a thousands separator. For thousands use 1 234 or 1’234, or write 1234. Fractions such as 6/2 are allowed. Answers must have the exact requested value; no rounding.
